# Experience design

Records of the design conversations about **what the mod should make a player feel**, kept apart from
the phase reviews because they answer a different question: a review asks whether the code does what
the corpus says, this folder records what the corpus has not said yet.

> **Nothing here is corpus.** A record decides nothing, is never frozen, and may not be cited as
> authority. Where a record and [`design/spec.md`](../spec.md) disagree, the spec wins. The owner's
> intent only becomes normative through the corpus's own route: an issue, a decision, an edit to the
> spec, a step in the plan, and a dated row in the brief's Appendix E.

## What belongs here

- **The owner's stated artistic intent**, quoted or closely paraphrased and dated. This is the
  material a future session cannot reconstruct from the code, so it is the reason the folder exists.
- **Proposals offered in the conversation** and what became of them — offered, accepted, rejected,
  or left open. A proposal that was not accepted must stay marked as one.
- **The open questions**, so the next conversation starts where this one stopped.
- **What the corpus already records**, with a site, when the conversation depends on it.

It does **not** belong here: a decision (that is the spec), a code finding (that is the phase
reviews), or a scene script (that is the codex's and the content author's work).

## Vocabulary

This folder does not borrow the corpus's markers, and does not reuse the phase reviews' either:

| Marker | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **[owner]** | the owner said it in session, on the date given. Intent, not decision |
| **[agent]** | a proposal made by the assistant; not accepted unless a later **[owner]** says so |
| **[open]** | undecided, and recorded so the decision is not lost |
| **[site]** | checked against the tree or the vendored game, with the site named |

## What this folder is not

- **Not a specification.** Nothing here may be built as if it were; the spec is where a buildable rule lives.
- **Not a transcript.** It keeps the intent and the state of each question, not the conversation.
- **Not a promise to the player.** An artistic intent recorded here can still be revised by its owner.
